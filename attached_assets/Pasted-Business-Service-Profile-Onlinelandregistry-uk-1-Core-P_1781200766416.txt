Business & Service Profile: Onlinelandregistry.uk
1. Core Purpose & What the Website is About
Onlinelandregistry.uk is a premium, independent UK property document search and retrieval portal. The platform acts as a digital intermediary to simplify the process of checking, compiling, and downloading official land registry records.

The Problem: Government systems can be slow, complex, and require manual lookups, especially if a property has no standard postal address (e.g., empty fields, forests, or building plots).
The Solution: A simplified, mobile-friendly multi-step booking wizard with automated postcode mapping, GIS coordinate mapping, identity validation, and instant PDF delivery.
2. Core Value Proposition (Why Customers Use It)
Although Land Registry records are public documents available directly from the UK government portal (gov.uk) for a base fee of £7, customers choose this platform for:

Advanced GIS Map Search: Ability to drop a pin on a map to look up deeds for land/plots that do not have a standard postcode.
Express Turnaround Priorities: Priority retrieval options (Standard, 4-hour Fast Track, and 1-2 hour Express dispatch).
Frictionless Delivery: Automated email delivery of compiled PDF packages, optional SMS alerts, and optional physical printed copies.
Complex Applications Made Easy: Step-by-step guidance for specialized filings like Deceased Joint Proprietor removal.
3. Business & Compliance Details
Operator Entity: Owned and operated by Swift Task Services Ltd (an independent service provider).
Government Affiliation Disclosures: The portal clearly states it is not affiliated with HM Land Registry or the UK Government.
Transparent Pricing Splits: In compliance with regulatory guidelines, the checkout summary explicitly breaks down:
Registry Official Document Fee: £7.00 per document (passed through).
Intermediary Service, Verification & Delivery Fee: The portal's handling fee.
Value Added Tax (VAT 20%): Assessed on the service fee portion.
